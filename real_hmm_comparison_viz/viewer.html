    What Alpha, Beta, Gamma Mean ?

    • alpha: forward evidence. After reading the prefix up to this step, how much probability mass reaches each hidden state?
    • beta: backward evidence. From this hidden state, how compatible is the remaining suffix?
    • gamma: posterior state belief. It combines alpha and beta: given the whole sequence, how likely is each state at this step?
    • xi: transition-pair belief between adjacent steps. This page focuses on alpha/beta/gamma because they are easier to inspect.

    What Is Learning, What Is Enforcing? ?

    DFA Hard verifier. It is compiled from graph constraints and answers whether a sequence is accepted or rejected. It does not learn probabilities.
    DomiKnowS-Aware HMM Probabilistic scorer. It learns or stores initial, transition, and emission probabilities over graph-compatible hidden paths.
    Together The DFA can reject invalid outputs exactly. The graph-HMM can rank, explain, train, or decode among legal paths using learned likelihoods.

    In this teaching demo the graph-HMM support is initialized from the DFA so illegal strings get zero HMM support too. In training use, the HMM parameters can learn which legal paths are more likely, while the DFA remains the hard correctness layer.
